# MOS Alpha-22 — Risk Engine

Risk is based on potential impact multiplied by uncertainty. The score changes the verification burden; it does not establish truth.

| Risk | Verification |
|---|---|
| LOW | STANDARD |
| MODERATE | ENHANCED |
| HIGH | STRICT |
| CRITICAL | HUMAN_REVIEW |

A critical claim must reach human review before publication. High-risk claims require strict verification. The engine is deliberately conservative and is not a substitute for domain-specific emergency protocols.

This phase contains no live emergency data and no publication capability.
