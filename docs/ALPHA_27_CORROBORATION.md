# MOS Alpha-27 — Corroboration

Corroboration measures whether captured evidence is mutually consistent; it does not establish truth.

## Independence rule
Evidence is grouped by `independence_key`. Repeated reports from the same underlying information do not inflate the independent-support count.

## Outcomes
- `CONSISTENT`: independent supporting evidence and no independent contradiction.
- `MIXED`: independent support and contradiction coexist.
- `CONTRADICTED`: independent contradiction exists without support.
- `INSUFFICIENT`: no usable support/contradiction signal.

Institutional and independent sources can both contribute evidence. Source category alone does not determine corroboration strength.
